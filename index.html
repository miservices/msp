How the Department is organized

Bureau

Field Services Bureau

Oversees uniformed patrol, highway enforcement, and specialized tactical operations conducted in the field.

Division

Field Operations Division

Troopers who patrol state highways and communities, respond to calls for service, and enforce traffic law.

Division

Commercial Vehicle Enforcement Division

Inspects commercial trucks and buses and enforces weight, safety, and hazardous-materials regulations.

Division

Special Operations Division

Houses the department's specialized tactical and emergency-response teams, deployed for high-risk or unusual incidents.

  • Emergency Support TeamA tactical response and crisis-negotiation team deployed for high-risk incidents such as barricaded suspects and hostage situations.
  • Aviation UnitOperates helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft in support of search and rescue, pursuits, and aerial surveillance.
  • Bomb SquadHandles explosive-ordnance disposal, suspicious-package response, and post-blast investigations.
  • Marine Services TeamPatrols the state's lakes and waterways, enforcing boating safety laws and assisting in water rescues.
  • Tactical Bike TeamProvides bicycle-mounted patrol for crowded events, campuses, and dense urban areas.
Division

Investigations and Intelligence Division

Coordinates major criminal investigations and the sharing of intelligence across local, state, and federal law enforcement.

  • Criminal Investigations UnitInvestigates major crimes that require specialized expertise or coordination across multiple jurisdictions.
  • Michigan Intelligence Operations CenterA fusion center that gathers, analyzes, and shares criminal and public-safety intelligence with partner agencies.
